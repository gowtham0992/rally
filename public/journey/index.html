If a payment fails

These are separate payments, not a provider-level atomic transaction. Rally does not confirm the group if settlement fails: completed captures require refunds and remaining authorizations require voids. Uncertain results remain visible until verified. A separate three-buyer test with real PayPal sandbox operations verified refund-and-void and lost-response recovery locally; that failure scenario has not been exercised on hosting.

This is engineering evidence from fictional accounts, not a customer pilot. No real friends or studios have been observed using Rally; willingness to hold studio capacity remains unvalidated.
