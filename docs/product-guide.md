# Use Rally

Rally helps a group find a pottery session, agree on pickup and approve each
person's own payment. The current studio is fictional and all payments use
PayPal sandbox.

## Start or join a plan

Open [Rally](https://rally.gowthamsarveswaran.com), sign in with your email and
start a group. Give it your name and share the invite in your existing group
chat. Friends join through that invite using their own email addresses.

Sign-in uses an email link, with no Rally password. Open the link in the browser
that requested it. If you arrived through a chat app's built-in browser, follow
the sign-in screen's browser instructions. A link opened in another browser may
require a fresh sign-in request. Requesting and opening links in separate phone
apps has not been independently validated across devices.

Your plans list shows where each plan stands. Inside a plan, the top card shows
your next action or what the group is waiting for.

## Add what works for you

Set your spending limit, available sessions, activity requirements and pickup
choice. A leave-by time is optional. Choose a must-have only when the session
really needs to include it; otherwise stay open to the published activities.

Your budget, availability and private note are not shown to other friends or the
studio. The organizer can see names and readiness, shared matching sessions and
payment-approval progress. “Has a step” means something still needs your answer;
saving details alone does not make every requirement resolved.

An optional AI draft can help interpret a private message. It proposes activity
or pickup choices, or asks one relevant question. Read its quoted wording and
check the form before saving. It cannot supply a spending limit, dates or another
person's agreement. You can use the form without requesting a model check.

If your choices conflict, Rally explains your own conflict privately. When each
person's choices work individually but do not overlap as a group, agree on a
change with your friends; Rally does not reveal anyone's private limits to blame
them for the mismatch.

## Ask a friend to pick up

A fired piece may be ready after you leave. Choose friend pickup, save your
choices and ask someone who is joining the plan. That friend must explicitly
agree. The request and arrangement are visible to the two of you and the studio,
not unrelated friends.

If they decline, choose whom to ask next. Saving a new preference revision ends
an existing pickup agreement, including a budget-only edit; the app warns you
before saving and the friend must agree again. Previous consent never silently
approves changed details.

## Handle a need outside the form

Keep any essential access, allergy or other unsupported need in your note.
Rally cannot verify capabilities the studio has not published.

For a published session that fits your form choices, you can privately ask the
studio inside Rally. Write the question yourself or explicitly request an AI
draft from your saved note. Review the quoted need and edit the question, then
separately consent to send it. Manual entry remains available without AI.
Your name, note and private form answers are not attached; avoid identifying
details in the question itself.

Return here for the studio's reply. Read every condition. A positive answer does
not clear the need until you explicitly confirm it covers every essential need
in your note for the exact session shown. A different day needs a fresh exchange.
Negative or discussion answers stay unresolved. The studio sees accepted
arrangements during booking review. [Studio questions](studio-questions.md).

When the catalog includes an external studio contact, the note-review panel also provides it.
After you actually confirm every need directly, you can record your confirmation
and consent to a generic notice for the studio. The notice does not attach your
name or note, and Rally labels the arrangements as unverified. The studio must
include them in its review. Editing your saved details requires fresh review.

An external confirmation requires a published contact; an in-app question needs
a configured studio operator and published catalog. If neither route is available,
or the studio cannot accommodate the need, it remains unresolved. Do not remove
an essential need or claim the form covers it merely to continue.

## Review the studio's offer

When everyone has finished their steps, the organizer confirms who's coming and
chooses a matching session. The current catalog supports groups of six to eight;
five people cannot book a six-person session. The organizer must also be joining.

The studio reviews the session, roster, pickup agreements, price, cancellation
terms and calendar time. Its approval reserves the session and opens a **two-hour
payment-approval window**. The offer shows your share and deadline in your time
zone. Read the exact terms before continuing.

Rally supplies invite and organizer reminder links for your group chat. It does
not send booking notifications automatically, so agree with your friends when
to return. Planning may be asynchronous; the approval deadline starts when the
studio issues its offer.

## Approve your share and check the result

Use your assigned Personal PayPal sandbox account for this release. Each friend
needs a distinct buyer account. Agree to the exact offer, continue to PayPal and
return to Rally. Rally verifies the provider's result; returning alone does not
establish approval or a completed payment.

An authorization holds your share. Rally starts collecting after all required
authorizations qualify in time. The booking is confirmed only after all required
charges are verified. Until Rally says the group is booked, it is not confirmed.

[Payments and privacy](payments-and-privacy.md) explains incomplete payments,
refunds and what to do when someone withdraws.

## Change your plans

Before an offer is issued, you can edit your own details. Changes invalidate a
pending studio request so it can review the updated plan.

During the approval window, use the offer's change controls to withdraw. This
closes that offer for the whole group. Recovery must finish before the organizer
can reopen planning; a new offer needs fresh payment approval from everyone.
The withdrawing person explicitly decides whether they are still coming. An
opt-out persists across later rounds until that person chooses to return while
planning is open.

Once settlement has started or a booking is confirmed, request studio review
under the published cancellation terms. Rally does not automatically refund an
individual absence, transfer a seat or reschedule a confirmed booking.
