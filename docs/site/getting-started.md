Rally helps you find a studio session with friends and pay only your own share.
Start with a group invite. Each person adds what works for them privately.

## Start a plan

[Open Rally](/) and choose **Start a group**. Sign in with your email, add your
name and copy the invite into your existing group chat. Friends join through
that link using their own email addresses.

There is no Rally password. Open your sign-in email in the browser that
requested it. If you arrived from a chat app, follow the browser instructions on
the sign-in screen before requesting a link. A link opened in another browser
may need a fresh request.

## Add what works for you

Set your spending limit, available times, activity requirements and pickup
choice. You can also add a private leave-by time.

Pick an activity requirement only if the session really needs to include it.
Otherwise, stay open to the studio's options. Rally checks the group's confirmed
choices against published sessions; it does not increase anyone's limit to make
a session fit.

Your spending limit, availability and note are not shown to friends or the
studio. Other friends see your name and whether you have a step to finish.

## Follow your next step

The top card in your plan shows what needs your attention. That might be a
pickup request, a choice to review, or your share of a studio offer. When you
have nothing to do, it says what the group is waiting for.

Return through **Your plans** to pick up where you left off. Use your group chat
for reminders; Rally does not automatically send booking notifications.

## What you can try today

The current release uses a fictional pottery studio and **PayPal sandbox**.
It does not sell real sessions or take live payments. The published studio
sessions support groups of six to eight people.

You can plan through the form without using AI. [How group booking works](/docs/group-booking)
explains the studio review and payment window. The Free host can take about a
minute to wake after being idle.
