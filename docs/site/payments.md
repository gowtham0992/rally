Each friend approves their own share through PayPal, directly to the studio.
The organizer does not collect a group balance or cover another person's share.

## Review the exact offer

Check the session, roster, your share, pickup arrangement and cancellation terms.
The studio opens a two-hour approval window, with the deadline shown in your
time zone. Your agreement belongs to that exact offer.

For this release, use your assigned **Personal PayPal sandbox account**. Each
participant needs a distinct buyer. There are no live payments. Returning from
PayPal alone does not establish approval: Rally checks the provider's result.

## A hold comes before a charge

An authorization is a hold on your share, and can affect available funds before
a completed charge. Rally starts collecting only after every required
authorization qualifies in time. The group is booked only after every required
charge is verified.

| What Rally shows | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Approval needed | Your share still needs your agreement and PayPal approval. |
| Authorized | PayPal has placed a hold. Your share is not necessarily charged. |
| Checking payment | The outcome is pending or uncertain; the booking is not confirmed. |
| Payment complete | Your charge is verified. The group still needs every required payment to complete. |
| Refund or release in progress | Recovery has started and still needs confirmation. |
| Studio action needed | An outcome needs investigation; it cannot count as success. |

## If one payment cannot finish

These are separate transactions, not an atomic group payment. If collection
fails after another charge completed, Rally starts a refund for completed charges
and releases remaining holds. Nobody takes over the missing share.

Refunds and released holds can take time. Uncertain results stay visible until
PayPal confirms them, and never count as a successful booking.

## When an offer closes

If a friend withdraws during approval, that offer closes for everyone. Someone
with no payment operations sees **You weren't charged**. Any payment activity,
including a pending or uncertain operation, retains the full payment status.

A new offer needs fresh approval. A previous payment approval cannot silently
authorize a changed roster, session or price.

## After the group is booked

Request studio review under the published cancellation terms. Rally does not
automatically refund an individual absence, transfer a seat or reschedule a
confirmed booking. Studio cancellation starts full refunds of completed shares
and releases remaining holds; results remain unresolved until verified.
