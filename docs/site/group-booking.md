A group plan becomes a booking through separate decisions: what works for each
friend, what the studio can reserve, and each person's own payment approval.

## Find a session together

Each friend adds their availability, spending limit and must-haves. Once everyone
has finished their steps, the organizer confirms who's coming and chooses a
matching session to send to the studio.

A private conflict is explained to the person who can resolve it. For example,
a required $72 wheel session with a $60 spending limit stays unresolved until
that person chooses what to change. Their friends do not see the private limit.

Sometimes everyone's choices work individually but do not overlap as a group.
Discuss the options in your group chat. Rally does not reveal private answers
or choose whose needs should matter less.

## Ask about a need outside the form

Keep essential access, materials or other unsupported needs in your private
note. You can ask the studio about a specific session in Rally, with optional
AI help to draft the question. Review the text and explicitly agree to share it.
Your original note and private form answers are not attached.

The studio replies in its own words. Read every condition and continue only if
the answer covers every essential need in your note for that exact session.
A reply that does not confirm the need leaves it unresolved. The studio sees
questions only when it next opens Rally; no notification is sent. An answer does
not reserve a place or approve a payment. [Privacy and consent](/docs/privacy)
explains who can see the exchange.

## Agree on pickup

Pottery is fired after the visit. If you cannot pick up your piece later, ask a
friend who is joining. They must explicitly agree. The two of you and the studio
can see the arrangement; unrelated friends cannot.

A declined request gives you a clear choice of whom to ask next. Saving changed
details ends an existing agreement and asks for fresh consent, including after
a budget-only edit. The app warns you before saving. An old agreement never
silently covers a new preference revision.

## Let the studio review it

The studio reviews the session, price, roster, pickup arrangements, cancellation
terms and time to block in its calendar. Its approval freezes an exact offer and
opens a **two-hour window** for everyone to approve their own payment.

The deadline appears in your time zone. Agree with your friends when to return:
the clock starts when the studio issues the offer. The organizer can copy a
reminder into your group chat.

## Handle a change of plans

Before an offer, you can edit your details. An edit cancels a pending studio
request so the studio can review the changed plan.

During the approval window, withdrawing closes that offer for the group.
Recovery must finish before planning can reopen. The person who withdrew
explicitly decides whether they are still coming; an opt-out persists across
later rounds until that person returns while planning is open.

The current studio needs at least six people. Five cannot book a six-person
session. The organizer must be joining too. New offers require everyone's fresh
payment approval. [Payments and recovery](/docs/payments) explains what happens
to existing holds or charges.
