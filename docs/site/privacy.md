Your planning answers help Rally find a session without becoming a group chat
about everyone's spending limit or personal needs. Shared status and private
answers stay separate.

## Who sees what

| Information | Audience |
| --- | --- |
| Spending limit, availability, leave-by time and private note | You and Rally's server. |
| Names, readiness and matching published sessions | Active group members. |
| Named payment-approval progress | The organizer, without account or transaction details. |
| Pickup request and arrangement | Its owner, nominated friend and the studio. |
| Your payment status | You; the studio has its merchant resolution view. |
| Text submitted for an optional AI check | You, Rally and the AI provider, Astropods. |

Other friends see **Has a step** when something needs your answer. They do not
receive the private reason or budget behind it. An organizer's role does not
let them waive your needs, volunteer a pickup friend or approve your payment.

## Choose what to send to AI

An AI check sends the message you submit and public studio facts. It does not
include other friends' preferences or saved budget and availability fields.
Anything you type in the message itself is part of that submission.

Read the disclosure before requesting the check. You can use the form without
AI. [How AI helps](/docs/ai) explains its limits and your confirmation step.

## Needs outside the form

Keep essential access, allergy or other unsupported needs in your note. Rally
cannot verify unpublished studio capabilities.

If the catalog provides a studio contact, the note-review panel offers that
route. After actually confirming every need directly, you can record the
confirmation and explicitly consent to a generic studio notice. The notice
attaches no name or private note, and says Rally has not verified the arrangements.
The studio's review includes them.

With no published contact, that path remains unavailable and the need stays
unresolved. The current fictional studio has no published contact. Do not delete
an essential need or falsely declare it covered to make the plan proceed.

## Changes need your decision

Only you can change your own attendance choice after opting out. A friend's
pickup agreement requires their explicit consent and does not transfer across
changed preference revisions.

The organizer and withdrawing person can see who withdrew from an offer.
Other participants see only that a friend withdrew. Existing offer and payment
records remain tied to that offer when a new planning round starts.

## Private text has an expiry

Unconfirmed drafts expire after thirty minutes. The default saved-note policy
clears raw notes and model quotations thirty days after planning ends, including
historical copies. Confirmed choices, consent, exact offer terms and financial
audit history remain available for reconciliation.

Deletion in Rally does not establish deletion by an external model provider or
its backups. Discarding a draft does not erase saved choices or payment records.
