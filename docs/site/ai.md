A message can be less precise than a form. Rally's optional AI helper proposes
activity and pickup choices, or asks one question when two meanings would lead
to different plans. You review and save the result.

## Clarify the meaning that matters

“I leave Friday” could mean you cannot attend a session, or that you can attend
but need a friend to pick up your fired piece later. Those answers can lead to
different matching sessions.

Rally can show a grounded question and preview what the answers would change.
It does not guess a volunteer. If you need a friend's help, you still ask them,
and they still decide.

A wish such as trying the wheel **if it is affordable** should not become a
hard wheel requirement. Explicit attendance followed by later travel is different
from inability to attend. The form remains available to check and correct the
proposed choices.

## Review before saving

An AI draft does not change saved preferences until you confirm it. Check the
quoted wording and every proposed field. A valid response schema does not prove
the model understood every essential need in a message; your review matters.

AI cannot verify access, materials or other studio capabilities. For an
essential need the form cannot cover, you can ask the studio privately about a
specific session. Write the question yourself, or explicitly ask AI to draft it
from your saved note. The helper quotes a need for the studio to answer; it does
not promise access, materials or safety. Review and edit the question, then
separately agree to send it. Your original private note is not attached to the
studio question.

The studio answers in its own words. You read any conditions and decide whether
the answer covers every essential need in your note for that exact session.
An unconfirmed reply leaves the need unresolved. If the accepted session no
longer fits the group, its owner privately sees which session the answer covers.
An expired question or answer requires fresh details and another studio review;
an old answer never widens to another session. Older discussion replies have
no in-app follow-up thread. AI does not
interpret the reply, reserve a place or approve a payment.
[Privacy and personal needs](/docs/privacy) explains the sharing boundaries.

## A small, bounded job

Astropods supplies model inference. The service receives the consenting person's
submitted text and public studio facts, without peers' answers or saved private
budget and availability fields. Studio contact details are excluded.

Activity and pickup drafts use a fixed vocabulary, exact source quotations and
at most one clarification. AI selects a literal need for a studio-question draft. Rally then checks the
question for known roster names, obvious contact details and financial wording;
the same checks apply to manual questions. Neither a grounded AI draft nor a private exchange guarantees a correct
interpretation or anonymous free text. Review every word before confirming or
sharing it. Manual question entry remains available without an AI call.

AI cannot supply money, dates, a nominee's agreement, a reservation or a payment
command. A deterministic planner checks confirmed choices and session-bound
studio answers. People create commitments; the model supplies a proposal.

## If the check cannot finish

Your work stays available for review. Provider failure has a different state
from a model result that needs your review: “AI drafting is unavailable” means
the service or response could not be used; “This draft needs your review” means
no usable question was proposed. Your note and typed question stay available
for manual entry. An interrupted request can stop appearing as pending without
starting another model call. Retrying is explicit and quota-limited, with no
automatic model retries. A changed preference, expired draft or late response
cannot confirm an older version of your choices.

The [verification guide](/docs/verification) distinguishes actual model checks
from authored fixtures and independent observations by people.
