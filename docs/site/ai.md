A message can be less precise than a form. Rally's optional AI helper proposes
activity and pickup choices, or asks one question when two meanings would lead
to different plans. You review and save the result.

## Clarify the meaning that matters

“I leave Friday” could mean you cannot attend a session, or that you can attend
but need a friend to pick up your fired piece later. Those answers can lead to
different matching sessions.

Rally can show a grounded question and preview what the answers would change.
It does not guess a volunteer. If you need a friend's help, you still ask them,
and they still decide.

A wish such as trying the wheel **if it is affordable** should not become a
hard wheel requirement. Explicit attendance followed by later travel is different
from inability to attend. The form remains available to check and correct the
proposed choices.

## Review before saving

An AI draft does not change saved preferences until you confirm it. Check the
quoted wording and every proposed field. A valid response schema does not prove
the model understood every essential need in a message; your review matters.

Unsupported needs remain unresolved. AI cannot verify access, materials or other
capabilities a studio has not published. [Privacy and personal needs](/docs/privacy)
explains the direct-studio confirmation path and its limits.

## A small, bounded job

Astropods supplies model inference. The service receives the consenting person's
submitted text and public studio facts, without peers' answers or saved private
budget and availability fields. Studio contact details are excluded.

The response uses a fixed vocabulary, exact source quotations and at most one
question. It cannot supply money, dates, a nominee's agreement, a reservation or
a payment command. A deterministic planner checks the confirmed choices against
the catalog. People create commitments; the model supplies a proposal.

## If the check cannot finish

Your work stays available for review. Provider failure has a different state
from an unsupported need. Retrying is explicit and quota-limited, with no
automatic model retries. A changed preference, expired draft or late response
cannot confirm an older version of your choices.

The [verification guide](/docs/verification) distinguishes actual model checks
from authored fixtures and independent observations by people.
