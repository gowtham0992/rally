# Blind comprehension check

Use this after the current README and recorded journey are available. It tests
whether the product is understandable; it does not predict an award or replace
observed friend/studio usability.

Give three people who have not seen Rally only the README’s first screen and
`/journey/index.html`. Do not explain the product or show the answer key first.
Ask for written answers, with one sentence per question:

1. Who is Rally for?
2. What can stop this group from getting a studio offer?
3. What question did the AI help ask, what did the person confirm, and what changed?
4. Why does the payment mechanism matter, rather than just splitting a bill?
5. What happens if the fifth person’s capture fails after someone else’s completes?

Retain the unedited answers, materials revision, date, time spent and whether
the reader was human or a model. Do not count this as demand validation. If
model readers are used, keep them in independent fresh contexts, identify the
models and obtain approved credits before making calls.

**Fail:** two of three cannot explain questions 3 and 4 accurately. Revise the
mechanism sentence and consequence preview; run the check again with new
readers before expanding scope. Two failed rounds trigger a framing review.
