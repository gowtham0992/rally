# Prepared test access

The normal app uses each person's own email and group invitation. The separate
[`/demo` entry](https://rally.gowthamsarveswaran.com/demo) provides a fictional
workspace with prepared friends and a studio identity for controlled testing.
It is not evidence of independent people signing in or coordinating a booking.

## Enter and keep your workspace

Use the access code supplied privately by the operator. Successful entry claims
an isolated workspace and managed session. Continue in the same browser; the
session and workspace carry the planning history you create. Opening another
browser can claim another finite workspace. Do not repeatedly enter merely to
reset a scenario.

Switching roles changes which fictional identity acts. A stale tab cannot act
as the newly selected friend without refreshing its identity. Each role retains
its own private preferences and permissions. Knowing a studio URL does not grant
studio approval rights.

Prepared identities and fixture preferences are labeled test setup. They do not
supply observed human consent, real merchant capabilities or PayPal approvals.
The [product guide](product-guide.md) explains the ordinary flow.

## External operations remain separate

Optional AI checks send the consenting role's submitted text to the configured
provider and consume credits and quotas. Sandbox checkout needs a distinct
Personal buyer account for each participant. Opening an offer does not complete
a payment. Never use real payment accounts, share credentials in source or call
simulated outcomes actual PayPal results.

## Operate prepared access

The entry requires the configured demo gate, stable private session key and
managed identities. The lease policy can have a fixed end for new sessions;
existing leases are not silently extended or recycled. Exhausted capacity must
be handled explicitly rather than overwriting old offers or financial history.

The [current verification record](../evidence/current-verification.json) contains
a dated capacity checkpoint. Available workspaces can change after that check.

[Provisioning history](judge-provisioning.md) records the scripts, identity
boundaries and earlier setup checks. Creating more persistent identities or
workspaces, changing access policy or replacing the published catalog is an
operator action outside normal application use.
