# Uncoached Rally study

Status: prepared, not run. Recruit no one and send no messages without the
owner’s authorization. Use real independent participants; models and scripted
personas are hypothesis generation only.

## Keep three outcomes separate

1. Can a new reader explain the product?
2. Can friends complete a booking without coaching?
3. Would they choose it over their normal group chat and reimbursement?

## Small study

Use two existing friend groups, one of six and one of seven. A six-person
studio cannot absorb a six-to-five dropout. In the seven-person group, one
participant later becomes unavailable, leaving six. Alternatively use a genuine
replacement; never pretend five can book or secretly reduce the catalog minimum.

Give the organizer the task of arranging a pottery session with their friends
without covering everyone’s bill. Friends respond as they normally would in
an existing group chat. Share realistic private budgets and available dates
individually, without naming the expected activity, UI control or answer.
Use fictional studio availability and distinct sandbox buyers, with no real
money. A team member may operate the studio, labeled as study setup.

The traveler may attend Thursday but needs somebody else to pick up the fired
piece. Establish that need explicitly in the scenario without directing where
to enter it. Being away for two weeks alone does not prove later self-pickup
is impossible. Do not instruct anyone to write “I leave Friday”, invoke AI or
choose a particular friend.

Before recruiting, configure an owner-approved SMTP sender: the current
built-in sender delivers only to project-team addresses. Do not work around
this by adding research participants to the administrative project team.

Use their own phones/browsers and emails, with sandbox buyer credentials for
checkout. First rehearse with team members through the actual WhatsApp/iMessage
invitation and email-link behavior on iOS and Android. Current Free sign-in
retains the same-browser PKCE requirement. Record failures rather than coaching
participants through them or claiming the instructions fixed them.

## Observe

- Sign-in success, browser transitions and infrastructure failures.
- Who knows it is their turn, the reminders copied, and each wait over 30 minutes.
- Visits required for pickup request and agreement; whether the nominee felt
  volunteered and understood the published pickup facts.
- Where needs are expressed: structured choices, note, chat or nowhere.
- Whether an AI question occurs naturally and reduces work when it occurs.
  No AI invocation is not itself a usability failure when the structured choices
  already cover the need. Two scenarios cannot establish that AI is never useful.
- Response to the seven-to-six dropout, including invalidated pickup agreements.
- Completion through studio approval, then each sandbox authorization within
  the studio’s actual window. Keep payment timing separate from planning timing.

Ask privately: who sees your budget; is the group booked; is there a hold or a
completed charge; what if another share fails; what exactly did you agree to
pick up? Avoid the ambiguous yes/no “has money left your account” question:
a hold can reduce available funds even before a completed charge.

## Coaching and thresholds

Any explanation of a status, instruction to return, direction to a control or
hint about the correct answer is coaching. Log it with a timestamp. Providing
sandbox credentials and repairing an independently verified outage are setup;
log infrastructure separately. Putting everyone on a call is a synchronized
checkout condition, not proof of spontaneous asynchronous coordination.

Directional success: both groups reach a studio request without coaching;
no unexplained pending-step stall exceeds 30 minutes; at least one group
finishes sandbox approvals in the window; every collector understands their
agreement; at least 10 of 13 participants distinguish a hold from a charge and
understand recovery. Report individual answers and all failures, not just a
pooled score. Small samples do not validate demand.

If both groups fail planning after these fixes, test organizer-proposed
published sessions with private friend responses before building more. If
friends prefer reimbursement despite organizer enthusiasm, reduce friend-side
work. Do not add agents or force AI interactions to manufacture sponsor fit.
