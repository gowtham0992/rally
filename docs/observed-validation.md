# Observe whether Rally helps a group agree

No human or studio results have been collected. This is a prepared protocol,
not evidence of demand. Obtain the owner's approval for named recipients and
the exact invitation before contacting anyone. Use consenting adults, fictional
budgets and sandbox-only tasks; never collect passwords or real payment data.

## Compare the same task with and without automatic clarification

The contract calls for eight counterbalanced group exercises. Recruit eight
groups of friends and assign four to structured-first and four to assisted-first.
Each group completes two equivalent pottery planning tasks on separate local
test groups. Use the same six-person roster size, catalog, identity steps,
collection controls and payment explanations in both modes. The assisted mode
may suggest a clarification; the structured mode must retain every manual control.
The existing `/groups/[id]?mode=structured` page selects that alternative.

Use two fictional collection scenarios with the same intended requirements:
“I leave Friday” and “I won't be around when the pieces are ready.” In both,
the traveler can attend the selected session but needs a willing group member
to collect after firing. Keep the published $48 hand-building option, $72 wheel
option and private $50 ceiling identical. Counterbalance scenario order as well
as mode. Do not give the correct collection interpretation to the organizer;
give the intended meaning privately to the traveler. Participation is voluntary.

Prepare and verify separate local test groups before each session. Do not use
the three remaining hosted judge workspaces for this research. Do not prefill
the two collection agreements or impersonate a human participant. Stop before
authorizing payments: this exercise measures agreement and comprehension, not
a new settlement proof. Record any moderator assistance as an intervention.

Measure from the first task instruction until an exact proposal is ready for
studio review. Record elapsed time, organizer interventions, abandoned steps,
incorrect proposals and whether both people understood and agreed to collection.
Keep equivalent confirmed facts and compare the resulting candidates; a
candidate mismatch is an engineering failure, not a user preference.

Before explaining the outcome, privately ask each participant:

1. Who can see your spending limit and private note?
2. Who agreed to collect the traveler's piece, and what did that agreement permit?
3. Has the session been booked yet? What still needs to happen?
4. How much would you approve, and when could it be collected?
5. What should happen if another person's payment cannot finish?

Use anonymous group IDs. Keep raw observations private, with permission for
recording requested separately. The results sheet needs one row per group and
mode: scenario, order, completion, elapsed seconds, organizer interventions,
correct agreement, comprehension, assistance and notable friction. No empty
row counts as a result; retain incomplete attempts and their reasons.

Report paired outcomes for the eight groups, including negative results.
The contract's six-of-eight commitment-understanding threshold guides product
changes; it is not a statistical conversion claim. If the assisted mode shows
no improvement in correct agreement or organizer effort, revise the interaction
or remove the user-benefit claim. A smaller pilot can identify friction but
does not complete this eight-exercise gate.

## Ask three studio operators about the actual commitment

After contact approval, show the reservation screen, exact six-person price,
setup/cleanup interval, two-hour approval window and compensation behavior.
Explain that the catalog and money are fictional. Ask the operator to describe
what they would block in their own calendar and what would make the arrangement
unacceptable. Then ask whether they would try it for one private session and
what condition would change that answer. Avoid asking whether they “love” the idea.

Record willingness, stated conditions, conflicting booking systems, cancellation
concerns and needed changes. An interview answer is stated willingness; only an
observed authorized reservation is evidence that a studio actually held capacity.
If none of three operators accepts the short hold, revise the workflow before
claiming merchant readiness. Do not reserve real capacity or agree to commercial
terms during an interview.
